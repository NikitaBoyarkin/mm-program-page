Customer churn is often perceived by the business as a single flat metric, which inevitably leads to wrong product decisions. Treating churn rate as an unambiguous evil hides the real dynamics: looking at the overall number, a company cannot see the difference between failed onboarding, a downgrade by loyal users and the natural departure of a non-target audience. The talk breaks down a research approach to deep churn segmentation that lets you find the true points of revenue loss in SaaS products.

The speaker will present a methodology for decomposing churn into nine key analytical cuts. You will learn how to properly cross-analyze cohorts, pricing plans, lifecycle stages and the behavioral patterns of churning users. The mechanics of working with qualitative data on reasons for leaving will be shown, along with ways to link it to quantitative product metrics. Special attention is paid to the concept of «good churn» — situations where losing a customer is mathematically and strategically good for the business, and attempts to retain them bring more losses than value.

Listeners will get a ready-made analytical framework for diagnosing churn. The talk will help move from passively watching an overall figure to targeted work with segments: you will start to clearly understand which users need to be won back by any means and which are better let go.
