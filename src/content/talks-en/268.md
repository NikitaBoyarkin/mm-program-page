How to assess the success of AI process adoption. Without clear metrics, AI transformation risks turning into blind hype that brings no measurable benefit to the business. The talk examines a systematic approach to assessing AI Adoption inside a large company: how to measure what many consider unmeasurable.

The speaker will break AI transformation down into specific areas and draw the boundary between what really lends itself to digitization and what cannot be measured. You will see how the analytics of AI Adoption at Nebius is structured from the inside: from solution architecture to a detailed breakdown of the methods applied. Drawing on the experience of successful corporate transformations in BI, Lean and Kaizen, the speaker will show not only working mechanics, but also break down his own mistakes, honestly highlighting situations in which standard approaches do not work.

As a result you will get real examples of metrics and analytics that can be immediately transferred to your own business. The talk will give product managers and CPOs a transparent framework for evaluating the effectiveness of AI initiatives, allowing them to manage the process of neural network adoption based on numbers rather than intuition
