A discussion among analytics leaders at major technology companies about the future of the function: which tasks will go away, which areas of responsibility will emerge and how to prepare teams for these changes.

- Should analytics take on more responsibility for the business result, and what authority is needed for that?

- How will the boundaries between analytics, product, marketing and operations management change? What new specializations may appear?

- Which models of organizing analytics teams will be in demand over the next three years?

- How will the requirements for analytics leaders change, and what should already be changed in developing the function now?
