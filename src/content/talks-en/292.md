AI has sped up analysis but not verification: an agent's answer appears within seconds, while understanding whether it is correct takes hours. In an end-to-end case — a single wrong answer about conversion — Victoria will show what context AI really needs, how the semantic layer is structured from the inside, where the boundary of agent autonomy lies and how to build proactive analytics on simple rules on that basis. The main takeaway: human judgment cannot be sped up, but it can be moved into the model — once, not in every answer.
