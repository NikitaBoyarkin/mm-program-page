When a site's ad inventory hits the utilization ceiling, the classic extensive monetization model breaks down. The business keeps demanding multiple-fold revenue growth by inertia, advertisers expect the usual volumes, but there are simply no free impressions left. An attempt to aggressively grow inventory leads to audience burnout and a drop in efficiency, while refusing partners leads to lost budgets. The talk covers Magnit AdTech's experience of managing a Retail Media ecosystem under a severe shortage of free capacity.
