The talk is about the transition from manual task specifications to a self-service architecture. The speaker will show how an analyst can independently develop a typical loading flow and ship it to production without the platform team being involved at every stage. The solution is based on YAML configurations, automated release pipelines and AI-based generators. Special attention will be paid to the mechanics of working with the neural network: what data, rules and documentation from the catalog are fed into the model's context so that the algorithm helps write valid integration configurations rather than just answering empty prompts.

Listeners will see the real picture of working with generative AI in data engineering. The talk will show how to scale the onboarding of systems without a proportional growth in manual labor. You will get a transparent scheme of how responsibilities are split: which technical handovers disappear thanks to platform automation, and which decisions on understanding a source, describing objects and controlling data quality will always require an analyst's expertise.
