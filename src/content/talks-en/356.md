Blind traffic buying due to delayed conversions is the main pain of performance marketing. The situation is aggravated by the fact that unconditional trust in mobile tracker (MMP) reports regularly leads to wrong optimization: ad networks hide fraud, take credit for others' achievements and openly cannibalize organic traffic. As a result, the budget quietly drains into channels that deliver cheap registrations but show zero payback over distance. The talk breaks down the experience of the dating service Mamba in building end-to-end ROAS reporting infrastructure, which made it possible to cut the performance budget by 30% while maintaining target profit.

The speaker will reveal the technical underside of the analytical loop that tightly tied network spend, clicks and real user payments into a single Single Source of Truth. You will learn how to build cross-validation of tracker data and introduce hybrid purchase management: routine protection against waste is handed to external API agents that automatically switch off unprofitable creatives based on early payback predictions. This cut reaction time to traffic drops from several days to a couple of hours.

Listeners will get a framework proven at Highload scale for cleaning the funnel of inefficient traffic. The talk will help marketing leaders and analysts restructure buying processes so as to completely free the team from manual budget micromanagement, focusing buyers exclusively on scaling profitable bundles.
