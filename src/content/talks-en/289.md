Dashboards stop being someone's job, while semantics and context have not yet become someone's job. The focus of BI has shifted from «a factory of reports as products» to governance, metrics and the knowledge graph. Self-service BI has been reborn as agentic self-service.

I will tell how at Авито we lived through 2026 in this fog, what we changed over the year and which expectations turned out to be overestimated. I will break down why certification of marts, metrics and dashboards has returned to focus and whether everyone can afford the prerequisites of an AI analyst. I will show how we grow the AI readiness of domains through federated data governance, metadata generation and a network of BI partners. There will also be problems that we cannot solve yet.

A talk without success stories, but with a therapeutic effect. It is for BI developers, leads and heads who do not yet feel new ground under their feet, those who are deciding what to do with BI in the AI world. You will compare your vision with the experience of Авито, take away working ideas and leave with the feeling that you are not running in the fog alone.
