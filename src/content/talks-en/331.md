Introducing AI agents into industrial analytics and Data Science processes requires a strict architectural base. If you hand a business task to a neural network without strict boundaries, the cost of a mistake can turn out to be critical. The talk examines the engineering scheme of an agent system based on Alfa-Bank's experience: from drawing up the task contract to managing agents, routing data and choosing tools. Special emphasis is placed on synchronization points — exactly how calculation results are transferred, in which cases the process is sent for automatic rework, and where the decision is made by a human with no alternative (human-in-the-loop).

The speaker will use real banking cases to show the mechanics of working with ambiguous metrics, the link between ML model quality and business KPIs and the impact of data drift. You will learn how to build a multi-level system of checks: from the validity of the source data and the applicability of the chosen method to the robustness of the final recommendations to the initial assumptions. Ivan will break down in detail the process in which the agent itself looks for logical contradictions in its conclusions before passing the result to a specialist for review.

Attendees will see how this architecture is embedded in Alfa-Bank's experimentation and Data Science platform. The talk will give data team leaders and DS engineers a ready-made framework for launching their own pilots, allowing them to accurately estimate the expected financial effect of introducing agents taking into account the costs of tokens, infrastructure and specialists' time.
