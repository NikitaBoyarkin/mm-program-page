We'll show how to build a fully autonomous calculation of a split experiment for key product metrics — WAU, MAU, NSAT/NPS, Retention and Sticky Factor, Contact Rate and CTR — without involving third-party analytical platforms. Traditional z-test and t-test formulas work only for simple click-through conversions, applying bootstrap requires computation time, and linearization and the delta method need to be applied correctly. A product's key metrics (active users, satisfaction scores, contact coefficients) have internal connectivity, dependence, autocorrelation, low frequency or ratio structure, so without bringing them to a certain form they give false conclusions. Source audiences in the groups may differ when testing different entry points into conversion funnels, which leads to a bias in group volumes.

We'll show how to calculate MDE and sample size for different types of metrics — calculation taking into account the metric type (observation dependence, internal connectivity, bias) and the multi-group Bonferroni correction (α-adjust). SRM control — a fast χ² test in SQL that reveals a skew in the distribution of users between experimental groups. We'll write, in plain SQL, the linearization of ratio metrics (CTR, Contact Rate) and calculate a t-test on the transformed metrics. We'll show how to carry out a correct calculation for ordinal data (NSAT/NPS) and special conversion metrics like Retention and Sticky Factor. We'll show a pipeline in Presto/Trino — from the raw hits table to a ready VIEW with confidence intervals and p-values. We'll tell examples where we encountered metrics unusual in structure and small data volumes, and how we handled experiments with different group volumes when testing different entry points into the funnels of user paths.
