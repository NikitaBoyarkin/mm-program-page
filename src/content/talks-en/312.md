Off-the-shelf analytics solutions like AppMetrica handle basic reporting well, but when moving to AI analytics they hit a natural barrier. For an autonomous LLM agent to answer ad-hoc business questions correctly without a data engineer involved, the company needs absolute control over the quality, naming and structure of raw logs. The talk breaks down the experience of the «Магнит Плюс» team in moving product event analytics from a third-party tracker to its own event stream.

The speaker will show the engineering underside of a large-scale event analytics migration. You will learn how to design the architecture of an in-house solution and ensure a seamless move without breaking current product dashboards. Special attention is paid to the process of automatic validation of the new data stream against the old one and to syncing the tracking logic on the fly.

The main focus of the talk is on preparing infrastructure for artificial intelligence. You will get a breakdown of the minimum requirements for event data: exactly how event structure and metadata need to be set up so that the neural network can confidently navigate the warehouse and give the business accurate answers under automated quality control instead of a human in the loop. Listeners will take away a roadmap for moving to their own pipeline and a checklist for data readiness for integration with LLM agents.
