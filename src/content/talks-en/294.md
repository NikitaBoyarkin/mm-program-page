The probation period was conceived as a legal tool to protect the business from hiring mistakes, but in practice it often turns into a legal trap. The situation where a candidate brilliantly sold themselves in the interview but completely failed the real tasks is familiar to every manager. However, an attempt to part ways with such an employee regularly runs into the resistance of the HR department, which blocks dismissal under the article because of the high risks of labor disputes. As a result, the hiring manager becomes a hostage of their own mistake and is forced to drag along an incompetent specialist. The talk examines the legal and management mechanics of safely parting ways with employees who have not passed probation.

The speaker will show how to set up the process of assigning tasks and control so that dismissal becomes a predictable procedure rather than a stress for the whole company. You will learn how to correctly formalize expectations in the first days of work, correctly record intermediate results and assemble an ironclad evidence base of non-conformity to the position held. Separate attention is paid to the algorithm for interacting with internal lawyers and HR staff: how to argue your position, remove colleagues' fears about inspections and formalize the parting without the slightest litigation prospects for the employee.

Attendees will get a strict management framework that allows turning the probation period from a paper formality into a really working filter. The talk will give leaders a clear toolkit for protecting their interests, so as to quickly and legally clean the team of unsuitable people without damage to work processes and the employer brand.
