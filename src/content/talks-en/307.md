The introduction of AI into marketing often comes down to point-by-point generation of images and texts, but the real breakthrough happens when agents take control of the full 360° contour of tasks. In this reality, strong marketers transform into operators of agents, multiplying efficiency many times over, and routine processes are automated. The master class examines the real experience of IT-Agency in a deep restructuring of marketing production with the help of AI agents.

The speaker will show the inner kitchen of the project: from organizing the Shared Workspace and designing the knowledge base (KPI, target audience, budgets) to integrating AI tools (Codex, Claude, Cursor) via API with advertising systems. We'll break down the architecture of end-to-end management: how agents run Yandex.Direct, launch SMM, generate content and design, build analytical dashboards and perform tasks on schedule. This is an honest engineering breakdown: in addition to the architecture and justification for the stack choice, you will learn about operational limitations, costs, the team's own mistakes and situations in which this approach absolutely must not be applied.

As a result you will get a step-by-step algorithm for moving marketing onto AI rails. The master class will give a clear understanding of how to set up the environment, connect channel APIs and restructure processes so that a small team manages the workload of a full-fledged department
