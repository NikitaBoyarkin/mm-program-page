In his lecture on game theory, Alexey Savvateev will explain the Nash equilibrium concept in accessible terms and show through a series of examples how the interaction of selfish participants can lead to a collective loss.

Braess's paradox, the prisoner's dilemma, the tragedy of the commons — in all these situations equilibrium logic does not guarantee a satisfactory outcome from the standpoint of the global benefit to society.

Particular attention will be paid to how changing the rules of the game changes people's behavior, which must be taken into account when designing incentive schemes.

No special mathematical background is required, but a willingness to reason will come in handy.
