The introduction of AI agents is changing the fundamental economics of the analytical function. It is no longer about simply speeding up SQL writing — agents take on the entire technical cycle: from data collection to building a report and writing a draft of conclusions. In practice this leads to a radical shift: if previously 80–90% of a specialist's time went to technical work and only 10–20% to communication with the customer, now this ratio is trending toward 50/50. The analyst stops being a bottleneck in report generation, but faces a new physical limit — the need to validate the many times larger volume of ready-made machine insights.

The talk, using the example of a real implementation of agent systems in two companies, examines the next inevitable stage in the evolution of data teams — the transition to an "insight factory", where the business can communicate with the analytical system directly. The speaker will break down the mechanics of transferring repeating corporate knowledge to the machine. You will learn how to digitize a finite volume of context (metric definitions, relationships between tables, access restrictions) so that the system autonomously gives reliable answers to typical questions.

Attendees will get a management forecast and a plan for adapting the backlog for their teams. The talk will help leaders and senior specialists understand how many people will be needed to support data infrastructure in the new paradigm, and how to restructure processes in order to direct the freed-up time to checking complex product hypotheses and creating new solutions.
