Т-Банк's A/B platform (Hippo) combines expertise in mathematics and work with internal data into a single fault-tolerant pipeline. Hippo includes Multi-CUPED (x2.2 speedup), experiment validity checks and sequential methods for early detection of broken experiments (x3.5 speedup at the cost of 1% power). We will discuss how to find the balance between adoption and quality in practice
