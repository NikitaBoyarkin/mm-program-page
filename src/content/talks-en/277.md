In B2B products and services with a long deal cycle, standard one-touch attribution models (first touch / last touch) predictably distort the real contribution of advertising channels. At M2, the hypothesis that such models were inadequate led to an attempt to implement multi-touch attribution. However, instead of a classic story of successful development, the speaker will walk through a case where building a multi-touch model initially failed — but it was precisely this failure that exposed fundamental problems in the traffic evaluation system.

Instead of weeks of designing custom algorithmic models, the team spent only two days on diagnostics using a standard stack: the Yandex Metrica Logs API and any DBMS. The talk examines the logic of this research in detail. The speaker will show the specific SQL query used in the study and will also share an even faster marker that your standard attribution models are evaluating sources incorrectly. This is an engineering breakdown of how to "cut the corner" and cheaply verify the problem without over-engineering and ML models.

Attendees will get a reusable analytical framework for quickly quantifying problems with one-touch attribution. The talk will show how the results of this short study at the level of raw logs forced the business to reconsider its strategy: to stop scaling classic performance acquisition and shift focus toward brandformance. You will take away a ready-made SQL template that lets you check the honesty of your current attribution on your own data
