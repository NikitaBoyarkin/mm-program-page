Moving to AI analytics is impossible without a single dictionary of metrics. If algorithms are let straight into the raw tables of the corporate warehouse, the business gets neural network hallucinations and discrepancies in key metrics. The foundation for data democratization is a semantic layer that translates business terms into strict logic that both LLM agents and ordinary employees can understand. The talk uses the example of the Careem superapp infrastructure to break down the process of designing such a layer from scratch.

The speaker will show how a micro-team of three people deployed an MVP of the semantic layer on the open-source Cube platform and proved to the business that the approach works. He will talk about the technical architecture of the solution and the nuances of integrating with Looker. Hamid will separately address the buy vs build choice: which tools are worth paying enterprise cheques for, and what is more profitable to build in-house (a comparison of Cube, Looker and Superset).

The main part of the talk is devoted to rollout under high load: operational constraints and technical difficulties that appear when the whole company gets access to data. Hamid will break down the process mistakes of the Careem team that any data function will face when connecting AI to a real warehouse. As a result you will get a blueprint for building a semantic layer where manual control by the data team remains only where automation cannot replace it.
