Market research, demand analysis, and competitive analysis still require a lot of manual work: formulate dozens of search queries, collect data from different sources, study competitors' websites, compare products and prices, and then pull it all together into a single conclusion. AI agents make it possible to change the very approach to this work. Instead of individual LLM queries, you can assemble a research workflow in which the agent independently formulates search tasks, calls external tools and data sources, analyzes the results, refines the search, and forms final conclusions. In the talk, practical cases will show how this works: from an agent with Wordstat MCP for demand analysis to a Deep Research agent for multi-step internet research and automated competitive analysis. We will also look at which tasks are already worth delegating to AI and where an analyst is still essential.

Listeners will get answers to the questions: how AI agents turn a chain of analytical steps into an automated process; how an agent can independently work with search demand data — forming queries, analyzing dynamics, and using the results to test product and marketing hypotheses; how a multi-step agent works that forms search queries itself, studies results, returns to search, and collects information from many sources; and how to turn typical competitive research from a manual process into a repeatable AI workflow.
