A beautiful idea: all orders fall into clear clusters. In reality there are no clusters, only a continuum of order purposes — not clouds at a distance from each other, but one complex figure with density clusters — «anchors».

We went through a hierarchy of basket representations and saw how these anchors manifest. And how their names drift: «Sweet gift» in June turns into a bag of charcoal for a barbecue, at graduation parties into chocolates, around New Year into alcohol. «For my beloved self» means toilet paper, cat litter and mosquito repellent.

We will tell you how to build such a model on your own data, which quality metrics to compute before A/B and why we did not turn it into a production tool. And where we went next: to the «occasion × product group» matrix and new levers for raising ARPU
