Alexey Lukatsky has more than 35 years in cybersecurity, 18 of them at Cisco and now at Positive Technologies. He has been an encryption developer, an infosec admin and an evangelist, so he understands perfectly why product analysts and security people often speak different languages. While the business demands fast insights and widespread AI adoption, the information security department responds with its usual "ban it".

The talk is about moving the use of neural networks out of shadow IT and into a legitimate channel. We will talk about how Russian companies actually solve the issue of working with Western LLM models without the risk of leaking trade secrets and sensitive user data.

Alexey will cover the engineering side of the question: how modern methods of anonymizing datasets before sending them to an external cloud work, what AI firewalls are and how they filter prompts and model responses. The talk will help analysts understand the logic of professionally cautious people from security, learn to discuss risks under tight deadlines and make a reasoned case for the architecture of their AI solutions before internal infosec review.
