Integrating external AI services into business processes inevitably runs into the harsh reality of data protection legislation (GDPR, 152-FZ, CCPA, and similar). Trying to legitimately use cloud LLMs, many data teams resort to obfuscation or pseudonymization of datasets before sending them via API. It is commonly assumed that replacing names with hashes and hiding explicit identifiers reliably protects the business from regulators. The talk breaks down why this approach is a dangerous illusion that often does not withstand either technical or legal scrutiny.
