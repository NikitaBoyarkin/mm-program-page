Representatives of large digital products share real cases of data classification and enrichment with ML/LLM. We discuss how AI helps automate business processes in Trust & Safety, cut costs and achieve a measurable economic effect.

- What new knowledge can be extracted from data already accumulated, and how do you turn it into a business result?

- Which Trust & Safety processes should be automated with AI, and which should not?

- How do you assess the economic effect of data classification and the return on AI investment?

- What difficulties do companies face when embedding AI into real business processes?
