Internal products — AI tools, telephony, sales scripts — require experimental validation, but classic A/B testing breaks down quickly here. Instead of millions of independent users you have dozens or hundreds of employees, where the test group talks to the control group and the results depend on team structure and incentive systems. The talk examines the experience of Avito Sales Tech in running A/B tests of internal products on small fixed samples (dozens of managers).

The speaker will show how to get a statistically reliable causal answer under strong, specific network effects. You will learn how to choose the level of randomization (employee, team or work period) and what price the move from individual to team-level testing carries.

We will go through working with small samples: group balancing, use of the pre-period, CUPED and validating the design on historical A/A tests. A separate block covers accounting for external factors: how to separate the learning period for a new tool from a lasting effect and how to factor in current incentives and motivation (KPI).

Attendees will learn to design informative experiments even on dozens of employees. The talk will provide a framework for evaluating internal AI solutions, CRM and processes: how to build a causal chain from a workflow change to a business effect. You will understand why access to a tool does not mean exposure, and how to correctly link growth in intermediate work metrics to the resulting economic benefit when deciding whether to scale a product
