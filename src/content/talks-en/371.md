Connecting an LLM to a database is not analytics yet: the agent does not know where the data came from, who has access to it and how the metric was calculated. We tell how AI was embedded into every step of the analytical cycle (loading, SQL, orchestration, catalog, dashboards) and what context it needed for that: automatically collected metadata, lineage, permissions at the platform level. We will break down where the agent already replaces manual work, where it makes mistakes and how to verify its answer all the way down to the source table. The experience of DataLens Platform, the largest update to DataLens: an AI-first platform where lakehouse, orchestration and a data catalog were added to the familiar dashboards in a single interface
