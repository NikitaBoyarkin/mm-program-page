The development of products like GigaChat sets an extreme pace: classic approaches to analytics simply do not work here because of the speed of change in the industry. To keep up with the product, the data team needs to radically reduce the Time-to-Market (T2M) of solving tasks while maintaining a strict quality bar. Moving an entire analytical vertical into an AI-native state solves this problem, but in practice runs into serious infrastructural barriers and psychological resistance from employees. The talk examines Sber's experience in systematically restructuring data analytics into an AI-oriented format.

The speaker will present a proven transformation fast-track that allows avoiding the classic mistakes of pioneers. You will learn what the target infrastructure architecture for an AI-native team looks like and which new competencies become critically important for analysts. A separate focus will be on change management: Evgeny will honestly tell how to deal with rejection of the paradigm shift inside the team, when employees refuse to trust agents and sabotage new processes.

Attendees will get a step-by-step trajectory for moving their departments onto AI-native rails. The talk will help analytics leaders and CDOs objectively assess the current maturity level of their infrastructure for entering such a transformation and understand what real economic effect and acceleration to expect at the finish.
