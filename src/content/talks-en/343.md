The global metric the business wants to grow and the local metric the product team can actually influence with a direct action are almost always two different entities. The gap between high-level KPIs and the results of specific releases means that local experiments consistently show growth while the overall economics don't add up. The talk covers the methodology for building a metric graph that connects proxy indicators to the main business goal and sets up a healthy process of iterating on hypotheses.

The speaker will show how to move from evaluating isolated A/B tests to analyzing an entire ecosystem of experiments. You will learn how the architecture of nested metrics helps extract hidden insights about user behavior that are unavailable from a point look at raw data. Special attention will be paid to the math of the cumulative effect: Max will walk through the algorithm for measuring and decomposing the impact of a large set of marketing changes on the final business indicator.

Listeners will get a rigorous analytical framework for evaluating complex product initiatives. The talk will provide a clear toolkit for situations where no single test can give a mathematically reliable answer at the macro level, but the business needs to estimate the real effect of the entire set of launched changes.
