When a model is trained on a complex mixture of web, images, audio or video, data preparation stops being a set of one-off scripts. It turns into a large-scale engineering product — a "data factory" with a continuous stream of raw sources at the input and a standardized training corpus at the output. The talk examines the construction of an end-to-end conveyor that replaces disparate scripts with a unified architecture for collecting, cleaning, deduplicating, filtering and packaging data for all modalities.

The speaker will break down in detail the structure of a multimodal pipeline based on distributed systems. You will learn how to control data quality at industrial scale without manual labeling, using extraction algorithms, automatic classifiers, scoring and heuristics. The audience will break down at which stages the cost of computation grows nonlinearly, and how to correctly prioritize sources so as not to spend unique resources on low-quality data.

As a result you will get a reference architecture of a pretrain corpus preparation pipeline, ready to be adapted to your tasks. You will see how to assess the labor intensity of processing different modalities already at the planning stage, combining strict data engineering with project management.
