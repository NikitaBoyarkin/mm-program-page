Hiring and onboarding junior analysts require huge investments of the mentor's and the team's time. However, a situation often arises when a technically trained specialist who has successfully passed the probation period suddenly loses motivation or leaves the company without having had time to recoup the resources invested in them. In the era of AI copilots, the value of basic SQL writing is trending toward zero, so the main challenge is to transform a newcomer from an operator of tools into an independent specialist who understands business context as quickly as possible. The talk examines the practical experience of Liga Stavok in building an analyst development path.

The speaker will break down real behavior patterns of novice specialists: from an avalanche of questions about every step to completely silencing problems. You will learn how mentors themselves unconsciously block the growth of juniors (when "it's easier to do the task yourself") and at what exact moment the motivation breaks. Separate attention will be paid to the mechanics of considerate feedback: how to discuss mistakes so that they accelerate the transfer of the area of responsibility, and how to build a step-by-step development plan for the first 90 days and the subsequent period.

Attendees will get a transparent framework for working with junior specialists. The talk will help leads and senior analysts reduce time spent on constant checks and micromanagement, correctly assess the effectiveness of onboarding (not only by the fact of closing the probation period, but also by the speed of transition to autonomous research) and retain grown talent within the team.
