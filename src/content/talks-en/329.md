We all discuss a lot about implementing AI in the IT SDLC. At the same time, about 30% of the labor intensity is taken up by data transformation tasks and the creation of data products. Finding data, assembling a mart, building a dashboard, checking quality, writing documentation — while these processes are performed manually, it is too early to talk about an AI-driven organization and autonomous processes.

I'll talk about how we organize end-to-end automation of typical data tasks: how the work of departments changes with the arrival of agents, and how we managed to achieve a 73% speedup on tasks performed by agents. Separately we'll discuss how to calculate the value of AI agents and what to focus on at the organizational level in order to move forward with the agentization of typical data work processes.
