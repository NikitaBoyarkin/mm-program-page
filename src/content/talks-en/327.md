Classic marketing long relied on metrics the business simply had to "believe", leaving a wide zone of brand and reach campaigns outside strict analytical evaluation. Today, attempts to improve old approaches point by point yield no result — the transition to fully measurable marketing requires not a smooth evolution but a radical restructuring of processes. The talk examines Avito's experience in implementing an AI-native framework that allows achieving transparent ROI where intuition and qualitative assessments used to rule.

The speaker will show the internal mechanics of Avito's new-generation marketing processes. You will learn how to build the architecture of AI-native marketing, digitize previously unmeasurable channels and connect creative initiatives with hard business indicators. Separate attention will be paid to the practical side of the transformation: the speaker will use concrete examples to break down exactly how algorithms change the team's routine tasks, and will share proven life hacks that already bring a measurable result.

Attendees will get a ready-made management framework for rebuilding the marketing function. The talk will give marketing directors, product managers and analysts a clear understanding of how to abandon "blind faith" in numbers and build a system where every ruble invested is tracked with mathematical precision.
