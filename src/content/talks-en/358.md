As a team grows, people management takes up ever more of a leader's time and attention. Hiring, employee development and organizational issues push out project context — it becomes harder for the leader to discuss tasks substantively and offer meaningful solutions.

We'll talk about how to distribute a limited context window between people and projects, what level of immersion is really necessary, and which practices help maintain an understanding of what is actually happening in the team.

Key thesis: a leader's resource is limited not only by time, but by the volume of context they are able to hold. The balance between people management and projects is achieved largely through managing one's own attention.
