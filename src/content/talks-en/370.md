In a year, measurements of brand visibility in neural-network answers have gone from an experiment to a line in the media plan. But there is still no single methodology: agencies, vendors and in-house teams define a mention differently, build the prompt sample differently, choose the number of runs differently and assess the stability of results differently.

On the panel we will compare the approaches of those who make, sell and buy these measurements.

We will look at which parameters can already be unified (and whether they can be at all), where methodological differences are fundamental, and what research on the stability of LLM answers can give practitioners.

The result is a draft methodological minimum for the market: a set of criteria for checking how well-founded the numbers in an AI visibility report are. We will put it together on the panel and publish it after the conference.
