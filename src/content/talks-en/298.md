A systematic approach in agentic coding: how to be sure this is a working product without reviewing tens of thousands of lines of code

The speaker will break down approaches to creating products with agents and show a working bundle of open-source AI coding tools. The solution is based on the OpenSpec development methodology and its improved version OpenSpec+. You will learn in which situations this bundle works and in which it does not, and why the main price for product quality is a multiple increase in token consumption.

Listeners will get a set of ready-made recipes for your harness, capable of rebuilding the stochastic process of AI code generation into a controlled engineering process.
