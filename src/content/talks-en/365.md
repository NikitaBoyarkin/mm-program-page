In the classic paradigm, data specialists often turn into a "live interface" to BI system filters, serving executives' urgent requests before planning meetings. Attempts to replace them with language models directly carry the risks of hallucinations and leaks of trade secrets. The talk examines the experience of fashion retailer Artenecy in deploying a conversational AI assistant that works on top of the corporate warehouse on a principle of strict separation of responsibilities: the neural network only translates a business question into a query, while the calculations themselves happen exclusively on the side of verified marts.

The speaker will lay out the architecture of a secure AI layer. You will learn how to implement seamless inheritance of access rights from BI so that each employee receives only the slice of data they are allowed, without taking information outside the corporate perimeter. Separate attention will go to the mechanics of building trust: every answer comes with a "number passport" (period, filters, source), and programmatic verification by code blocks incorrect outputs. Using a real mistake by the model, it will be shown how the hallucination protection system works and how to properly set pilot quality metrics, moving away from abstract "accuracy".

Attendees will get a blueprint for moving to AI-native analytics, where the log of user questions becomes an automatic sensor of demand for new marts and the basis for a metrics dictionary. The talk will help data team leaders restructure processes so that the analyst stops being a courier delivering numbers and becomes the architect of a system that generates business hypotheses.
