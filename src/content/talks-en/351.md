Analyzing agent-loop transcripts is a key skill for working on the quality of AI products. In-house evals are often more important than public leaderboards: a 10% quality gain on a state-of-the-art model can lead to a degradation of user experience. Listeners will learn how to apply LLM to highly non-deterministic tasks (in our case it is a city walk, but the general approaches — your own eval, working with transcripts, fine-tuning tools — can be applied anywhere) and will learn to balance the agentic and deterministic parts of a product
