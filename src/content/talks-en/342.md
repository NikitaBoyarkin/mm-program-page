We discuss identifiability, bias and decision-making under uncertainty. Bayesian non-linear econometrics makes it possible to estimate causal relationships, and how to build such a model will be the subject of our conversation:

how to construct a model so that it is not only interpretable but also identifiable, how to parameterize it so that it doesn't fall apart during estimation and the bias is overcome, how to make decisions under uncertainty, and how stochastic optimization theory can help us.
