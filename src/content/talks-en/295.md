The drive to take routine load off analysts (ad-hoc queries, incident investigation) often runs into the complexity of setting up and maintaining AI tools. In the Uzum Market case, the team came to use Opencode as a tool that covers 90% of routine tasks with minimal effort. As a result, every stakeholder got a "$100-a-month junior analyst": just enter credentials to the needed systems, and all permitted skills, MCPs and models appear as a single web interface.

The talk examines in detail the technical implementation and architecture of such an AI Harness: how to deploy it on a small server in just a week and ensure full controllability. The speaker will show the transformation of work processes: Uzum analysts no longer write SQL queries by hand, 90% of dashboards are created by agents from scratch (from a Jira task to the final visual), and investigating DAG failures takes minutes. Scaling mistakes, operational limitations and tasks where LLMs absolutely must not be left unchecked will also be examined.

Attendees will learn how to choose and configure scaffolding and MCP servers to automate routine work. The talk will give concrete advice on introducing the tool into teams and help analysts move into the role of leaders of mini-teams of AI agents, multiplying the speed of qualitative research.
