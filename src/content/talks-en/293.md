The product analytics market often seems overheated, and the employment process — opaque. Candidates face a string of rejections, inexplicable salary spreads on identical positions and strange hiring conditions. The talk examines the underside of job hunting in IT: without abstract advice and stories of "successful success", exclusively on the basis of real interviews, salary negotiations and one's own mistakes.

The talk examines in detail the process of evaluating vacancies and employers. The speaker will explain why financial conditions on similar positions differ drastically, what actually hides behind loud job titles, and how to stand out among competitors. The audience will break down the typical screw-ups of hiring companies and the non-obvious red flags one can encounter in an interview, including specific cases from big tech.

Attendees will learn how to objectively assess their value on the market and not measure professionalism by the number of rejections. The talk will give a list of questions about tasks, processes and conditions that must be asked of the hiring manager before accepting an offer, in order to choose a job by real substance rather than by the signboard.
