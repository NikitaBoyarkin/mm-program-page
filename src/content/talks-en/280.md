Media advertising has historically suffered from fragmented data: classic analytics tools struggle to connect upper-funnel impressions with final conversions, especially when the user journey crosses web and mobile apps. When the old system's architecture stops coping with cross-platform tasks and strict attribution limits, the business faces a choice: endlessly refactor the legacy or design the base from scratch. The talk covers the experience of the Yandex team, which chose the radical path of rebuilding the architecture.

The speaker will break down the design of the new data model in detail and show how abandoning the old logic made it possible to implement a truly flexible attribution window. The main focus of the talk is the mechanics of stitching disparate signals into a single user journey. You will learn how, under the hood, a seamless link is built between raw media impressions, goal actions from classic Metrica, and in-app events from AppMetrica, providing conversion transparency regardless of platform.

A separate block will be devoted to extending the analytics toolkit on the new architecture. Listeners will see how the built-in flexibility allows enriching data with custom parameters and introducing advanced traffic-quality metrics. The talk will help data team leads make informed decisions about rebuilding internal analytics platforms, and will give analysts an understanding of how to transparently assess the impact of top-of-funnel campaigns on final revenue.
