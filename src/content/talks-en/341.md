Where do personal data appear in an LLM product? How an external API, a Russian intermediary and self-hosting an open model differ. How to establish the actual recipients of data and the countries of processing. Legal grounds for processing, localization, notifications under Articles 12 and 22, contracts and the allocation of liability. What information you need to obtain from the vendor and what to do if it does not disclose the necessary terms. What happens to the legal status of data after full names and phone numbers are removed. How to account for indirect identification, a retained mapping table and the possibility of restoring information from context. Storage of requests and responses, use of data for training, employee access, engagement of other processors, deletion of information and actions in case of incidents.

Walkthroughs of typical product scenarios and edge cases (for example, the model is hosted inside the company, but individual components — speech recognition, embedding generation or logging — use external services. How to assess the system as a whole in this case)
