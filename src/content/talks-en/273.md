A/B tests help make product decisions, but average experiment results do not always reflect what is happening with individual user groups. In the talk we will look at a content platform case and show how a deeper look at the audience helps find unexpected experiment effects and make more precise decisions about product development. And also how this affects the way content that suits a particular user profile is recommended
