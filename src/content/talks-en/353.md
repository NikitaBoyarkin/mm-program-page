A talk about building a system for evaluating advertising effectiveness based on Marketing Mix Modeling. Using the X5 Digital approach as an example, we look at how to combine advertising and business data, choose a model, evaluate a future campaign and compare budget allocation options in a single service. Data preparation, the model and the user process work together. This approach helps consistently evaluate and compare media plans while preserving the model's constraints and human responsibility for the final decision
